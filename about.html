About Faaiza Munir

British author writing character-led cosy crime, complicated family hierarchies, and mysteries driven by humour, warmth, and relentless sisterly interrogation.

Faaiza Munir is from Glasgow, Scotland but lives in London with her husband, three children, and a very unbothered cat. She spends her summers roaming around Scotland with her family, climbing mountains and kayaking and swimming in lochs.

She has been a crime fiction fan ever since she read her first Agatha Christie novel at 14 years old. She studied English Literature at University and loves reading all genres, but some of her favourite authors are Agatha Christie, P.G. Wodehouse and Anthony Horowitz. Her debut novel, “Arranged Murder”, is out in March 2027.
