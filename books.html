Books & Writing

Explore the world of The Asghar Family Mysteries: witty, warm, character-led crime fiction starring four sisters who solve murders while managing family matrimonial expectations.

Debut Novel • Book 1

Arranged Murder

Four sisters. One dead body.
And a hunt for the perfect husband.

When a distinguished gathering of British-Pakistani families convenes at a suburban estate to discuss matrimonial prospects for the eldest daughter, the evening is abruptly shattered by the discovery of an unexpected corpse in the conservatory.

With the local constabulary baffled and the prime suspects including three exceedingly eligible bachelors and one notoriously meddlesome auntie, the four Asghar sisters realise that unless they find the real killer themselves, the wrong man will take the fall—and wedding season will be ruined forever.

Suspects & Sleuths

Meet the Four Asghar Sisters

Four different temperaments, four conflicting investigative theories, and an unbreakable family bond.

The Eldest • The Planner

Amina Asghar

Chronically responsible management consultant with a colour-coded spreadsheet for every crisis. She treats murder investigation like a restructuring project and has zero patience for alibis that fail basic arithmetic.

The Diplomat • The Stylist

Zara Asghar

Fashion stylist whose disarming charm and eye for sartorial detail make suspects spill their deepest secrets without realising they are being cross-examined over Earl Grey tea.

The Academic • The Cynic

Nadia Asghar

PhD candidate with a deep fascination for forensic pathology and an encyclopaedic knowledge of historic British poisons. She was the first to notice the conservatory lock had been picked from the inside.

The Wildcard • The Listener

Shireen Asghar

The youngest sister, university student, and family eavesdropping champion. Quiet in public, lethal in private WhatsApp group chats, and keeper of everyone’s most embarrassing secrets.
