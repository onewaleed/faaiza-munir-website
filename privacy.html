Privacy Policy

The short version: this site doesn't track you, doesn't use cookies, and only keeps the details you choose to send.

Last updated: November 2026

Who looks after your information

This website belongs to Faaiza Munir, who is responsible for any personal information collected through it (the “data controller”). The site is built and run for her by WIZN.MEDIA (IWN GLOBAL LTD).

Questions about your information? Email info@faaizamunir.com.

What we collect, and why

If you send a message

The contact form asks for your name, email address, the kind of enquiry and your message. We use these only to read and reply to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages people choose to send.

If you join the Reader Club

The newsletter sign-up asks for your email address. We use it only to send Faaiza's newsletter: book news, cover reveals and event updates. You'll be asked to confirm before you're added, and every email has an unsubscribe link. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

When you simply visit

We use privacy-friendly visitor statistics to see things like how many people visit and which pages are popular. It uses no cookies, doesn't track you across other websites and doesn't identify you. Like any website, our hosting provider briefly processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the site secure.

Cookies

This website doesn't set cookies, so there's no cookie banner. If that ever changes, we'll update this page and ask for your permission first where the law requires it.

Who else handles it

We use trusted providers for website hosting, our newsletter and email. Some are based in the US, and your information is protected by UK-approved safeguards. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Messages: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, or ask us to stop using it. To do any of this, email info@faaizamunir.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
