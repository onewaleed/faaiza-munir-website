Murder, Matrimony, and Six Pots of Chai

Born in Scotland to Pakistani parents, Faaiza Munir grew up navigating two distinct cultures with one constant companion: an insatiable appetite for murder mysteries.

She quickly observed that the investigative methods used by Golden Age detectives were remarkably similar to the interrogations conducted by aunties at British-Pakistani family weddings: polite on the surface, forensic in detail, and utterly inescapable.

“In my family, nobody solves a problem quietly. We solve it over six pots of chai, three conflicting theories, and someone inevitably pointing out that the prime suspect was poorly dressed anyway.”
