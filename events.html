ON THE FESTIVAL CIRCUIT • THE CAPITAL OF CRIME FICTION

Dispatches from Harrogate

To authors, publishers, and crime fiction fans across the globe, the Theakston Old Peculier Crime Writing Festival is known simply by one word: Harrogate. Hosted every summer at the historic Old Swan Hotel in North Yorkshire, it is the beating heart of the British mystery scene.

Festival Grounds

The Old Swan Marquee & Grounds

The landmark giant orange "READ" installation welcoming crime writers and avid mystery readers onto the festival lawns at the historic Old Swan Hotel.

The Official Welcome

Theakston Old Peculier Crime Festival

The official entrance banner and box office, bearing the legendary crimson fingerprint motif of the world’s foremost crime writing gathering.

The Host Town

The Town of Harrogate

The colourful town mural in Harrogate, North Yorkshire—celebrating the beloved spa town known to mystery writers simply as ‘Harrogate’.
